Government Letter on Language Policy — East Pakistan
📅 26 January 1952 · National Archives of Bangladesh

This classified government communiqué outlines the official policy of imposing Urdu as the sole state language, revealing administrative indifference to Bengali cultural identity prior to the Ekushey protests.

DOC
Field Report: Agartala Conspiracy Case Proceedings
📅 1968 · Intelligence Bureau, Pakistan

Declassified intelligence reports show the Pakistani government's strategy to discredit Sheikh Mujibur Rahman. Cross-referencing these records exposes significant contradictions in the prosecution's narrative.

IMG
Proclamation of Independence — Bangladesh, 1971
📅 10 April 1971 · Mujibnagar Government

The formal Proclamation established the legal framework for the liberation struggle and appealed for international recognition of the new state of Bangladesh.

History Archive is an independent academic project dedicated to the preservation, analysis, and dissemination of historical knowledge — with a particular focus on the history of Bangladesh and South Asia from 1947 to 1971.

This portal was created as a scholarly resource for students, educators, and researchers. It brings together primary source documents, annotated research articles, and oral history testimonies.

Affiliated with the Department of History, Jahangirnagar University
Primary source documents from the National Archives of Bangladesh
Oral history narratives collected through field interviews across Bangladesh
Explore the Archive →

📚 Academic Scope

Coverage spans Bangladesh history, South Asian history, European modern history, and Russian & Central Asian history — with depth across the 19th–20th centuries.

🎙 Oral Histories

Over 32 oral history testimonies — many from women participants — documenting first-hand accounts of the Language Movement and Liberation War of 1971.

📄 Primary Sources

Declassified government documents, intelligence reports, and official proclamations from the National Archives provide the evidentiary foundation for all research articles.
